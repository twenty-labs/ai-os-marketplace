# Journey stations

Journey mode walks these stations in order as a brand-new user. Adapt the names to the product (a web product's "store listing" is its landing page and search result), but never drop a station — every station is a row in the report.

**Scoring.** Each station's score = round(5 × questions answered "yes" ÷ questions asked). A red-flag sign caps the station at 2. A missing evidence source (code, running product, external config) caps it at 3 and marks it unconfirmed. Two audits with the same evidence must produce the same scores.

At every station also ask: **when real users arrive, will this step be measurable?** Name the event that must exist and check that it fires with the properties needed to segment it.

## 1. Store listing or landing page
- Do the first three seconds say what the product does, for whom, with one reason to believe?
- Is the first screenshot or hero an outcome, not a login screen?
- Is there social proof (ratings volume, reviews) and nothing that contradicts the product?
- Red flag: the listing describes features that no longer exist, or is not in the audience's language.

## 2. Landing to install or signup
- One primary call to action?
- Do campaign parameters survive into the product (tracking links, deferred deep links)?
- Is web analytics installed, with consent where required?
- Red flag: no measurement on the page — every ad that lands here is blind.

## 3. First open to account
- Can value be tasted before an account is demanded?
- Is the fastest sign-in method first; does email verification have a resend and a way back in?
- Red flag: a hard wall at sign-in with no taste of value first.

## 4. Onboarding and setup
- Does each step say why it asks? Can steps be skipped? Does progress never go backwards?
- Do permission requests come after value, with a fallback when refused?
- Red flag: an OS permission prompt before anything has been taught or shown; a mandatory step with no exit.

## 5. First session
- Can the first session not fail? Is it short? Does it end with a concrete achievement and a one-tap next step?
- Is the first unit of content or work non-empty on the current build?
- Red flag: the first item opens empty, or the first session can end in failure.

## 6. Day-2 return
- Is a reason to return planted on day 1 (progress, a goal, a specific promise for tomorrow)?
- Is notification permission asked after first value; do messages carry personal progress and deep-link to the right screen?
- Red flag: the same message for everyone, or a tap that goes nowhere.

## 7. Limit reached → paywall
- Does the free tier let users taste the paid value a few times?
- Does the paywall appear at a moment of achievement more often than at a moment of frustration?
- Do users know the paid plan exists before they hit a limit?
- Red flag: the first time a user meets the paid plan is an error message.

## 8. Paywall → purchase
- Does the headline state an outcome? Is the free-versus-paid comparison clear; are trial terms and the post-trial price printed?
- Is the annual plan's saving marked; is there an exit that is visible but not louder than the call to action?
- If the store or billing returns no products, does someone get alerted?
- Red flag: no purchasable products in the test environment, or a displayed price that does not match the store.

## 9. After purchase
- Does the success screen lead straight back into the product?
- Does subscription management show the renewal date; does cancellation ask why?
- Are offers, gifts and win-back paths reachable and measured?
- Red flag: nothing changes in the product after paying.
