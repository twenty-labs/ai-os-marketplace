# Generated asset review checklist

A person reviews every generated asset before it ships; the checklist makes the review consistent. Reject rather than fix by hand, unless the fix is a recorded post-processing step.

## Every asset
- It does what was asked: the right object, scene, word or line.
- It is not a duplicate of an existing asset.
- Its ledger entry is complete (input, engine, voice or style version, cost, licence).
- Nothing in it identifies a real person, brand or copyrighted character that was not cleared.

## Images, illustrations and icons
- Matches the style anchor: line treatment, shading, palette, proportions, texture. Put it beside two existing assets of the same set and compare at real display size.
- No unwanted text, letters, numbers, watermarks or signatures. Prompts that forbid text are not reliable; look.
- Brand marks are composited from the real file, not drawn by the generator.
- No anatomical or physical errors (hands, faces, perspective, objects merging).
- Works at the sizes the product uses: silhouette readable small, no detail lost at icon size, transparent edges clean.
- Colours sit within the palette design-surface records; contrast holds on the backgrounds it will appear on.
- File format, dimensions and size match the project's optimization step.

## Audio and voice
- The text spoken is exactly the text in the ledger: no skipped, repeated or invented words.
- Pronunciation is correct, especially for the taught language, names and numbers; checked by someone who speaks the language.
- The voice matches the role and the rest of the set; volume and pace are consistent with neighbouring clips.
- No clipping, noise, long leading or trailing silence, or truncated endings.
- Delivery tags (whisper, cheerful) produced the delivery intended and were not read aloud.
- Format, sample rate and loudness match the project's convention.

## Children's products
- Nothing frightening, violent, sad without purpose, or suggestive, including in backgrounds and facial expressions.
- No real-person likeness; characters are original.
- Text in images, where any is allowed, is large and simple.
- No recording of a child's voice is stored or reused.
